The World's Most Valuable Advertising Still Can't Tell You If It Worked

Apr 15, 2026·3 min read
Published by Lawrence Kiige

Over the past year, I've spent a lot of time speaking with media owners across Africa. But despite all differences, one thing kept coming up. An industry that moves millions every year is still being managed with spreadsheets, WhatsApp conversations, phone calls and manual reports.

Inventory lives in one place while campaign updates live somewhere else. Proof of flighting is shared over email, and reporting still takes hours to prepare manually. Teams spend more time following up than moving work forward. For an industry that's been around for decades, it felt like we had accepted this as the normal way of working.

Digital advertising has spent years becoming measurable. Every campaign can tell you how it performed, what was spent and what happened. Offline advertising has always delivered value too, but the infrastructure around it hasn't evolved at the same pace. I believe that's one of the biggest opportunities in our industry today.

That's why we started building ADWrap.

We're not trying to replace the relationships that make offline advertising work. We're building the infrastructure that helps those relationships work better. A place where media owners can manage inventory, proposals, campaigns and reporting from one platform, while giving advertisers greater visibility into the campaigns they're investing in.

We started with Out-of-Home advertising because that's where we've spent our time learning. We've sat with media owners, listened to their frustrations, watched how they manage their businesses and challenged many of our own assumptions along the way. Every conversation has shaped the product we're building today.

One lesson has stayed with me throughout this journey: building software is the easy part. Understanding an industry well enough to build something people genuinely rely on is much harder.

Many of the features we've shipped today aren't the same ones we first designed. Customers challenged them. The market challenged them. Sometimes we realised we were solving the wrong problem. So we rebuilt them. We'll probably rebuild many more, because I'd rather build what the industry actually needs than become attached to our own ideas.

While we're starting with Out-of-Home advertising, our ambition has always been much bigger than billboards. I believe every traditional advertising channel deserves the same level of visibility, collaboration and accountability that digital advertising enjoys today. Whether it's radio, television or whatever comes next, the goal remains the same: make offline advertising easier to manage, easier to measure and easier to trust.

We're still early, and there's a long road ahead. But we're clear about where we're going. The future of offline advertising shouldn't rely on disconnected tools, scattered communication and manual processes. It should be built on better systems, better data and better collaboration for everyone involved.

That's the company we're building at ADWrap. And this is only the beginning.
