Bridging the gap between media spend and broadcast reality

Radio remains one of the most powerful and heavily funded advertising mediums in emerging markets, yet it operates as an unverified black box. Brands and agencies spend millions buying airtime, but rely on manual, self-reported "Run Logs" generated by the radio stations themselves to verify execution.

AirProof introduces independent, automated media auditing to broadcast radio. By utilizing Automatic Content Recognition (ACR) and audio fingerprinting, AirProof listens to broadcasts 24/7 and definitively tracks when and where an ad was played, replacing analog trust with digital-grade analytics.

Stream Ingestion

Continuous server-side recording of online streaming feeds from target radio stations. (For non-digital stations, localized edge nodes with FM receivers capture and upload raw audio).

ACR Fingerprinting

When a brand uploads a 30-second ad (MP3/WAV), the engine creates a unique audio spectrogram signature.

Resilient Matching

The engine scans continuous streams 24/7, designed to identify the exact ad signature even through static, DJ voiceovers, or signal compression.
